Use case: stylized-concept.
Create ONE polished exterior concept image for a second space station in the game Space Trucking. Landscape 1536x1024 composition, a complete legible three-quarter front-and-above view of a compact orbital freight and resupply depot in black space, very small distant icy moon at edge. This is a NEW station design, not the previously approved long elevated industrial keel with twin process drums.
Art direction: grounded cassette futurism, NASA industrial hardware, cold-war space equipment. A refined buildable Blender game asset: clear blocky manufactured shapes, small chamfered edges, modest polygon counts on cylindrical parts, restrained panel seams and mechanical detail. Convincing physically assembled engineering, approachable workaday atmosphere. Warm ivory pressure hulls, muted maritime blue identification bands, graphite frames, small ochre safety markings, warm white practical lights. No giant futuristic glass buildings, no fantasy spikes, no weapons, no neon, no giant circular city, no ornamental empty platforms.
Architecture: squat broad modular twin-bay depot. TWO equally large, genuinely full-depth rectangular enclosed hangar modules mounted side by side along a short rear service spine, structurally connected to a compact stepped operations block and three short cylindrical habitation/utility modules above and behind. A small cluster of spherical-ended water/fuel tanks at the rear, exposed purposeful piping, maintenance panels, antennas, separated vertical dark radiator panels and two modest solar wings extending sideways/backwards clear of both flight approaches. Make the upper massing compact and asymmetric; avoid the long linear silhouette of the other station.
CRITICAL gameplay dimensions and arrangement: each hangar houses the SAME existing standard interior, 28 metres wide by 52 metres deep by 10 metres clear height, with an 18 metre wide by 9 metre high front doorway. Show the full volume extending back from each door, not shallow boxes. External hull roughly 31m wide by 56m deep. A stout 5m separation/service section between hangars keeps straight parallel approach paths independent. BOTH flight openings face the camera and are completely unobstructed by booms, pipes, platforms, solar panels or radiators. BAY 01 is reserved for player traffic indefinitely; BAY 02 is for NPC freighters. Clearly stencil only '01' and '02' on their respective doorframes, with small 'BERTH' labels; no other prominent text.
One door closed or nearly closed and the other fully raised into an engineered header pocket, showing a real lit hangar interior with cream/red wall equipment, clear landing floor, cargo pickup/delivery markings to the sides, a dispatch terminal counter against one wall. A small blocky ivory cargo freighter with 4 recessed aft thruster housings is aligned straight in front of the open NPC bay approaching nose-first, leaving its doorway unobstructed and establishing believable scale: ship 10.9m wide and 26.5m long, bays significantly larger than it. No exposed people in vacuum.
Lighting: crisp but gentle solar key from upper left, readable reflected fill, local warm light around bay doors, manufactured surfaces with very restrained wear, subtle bevel highlights. High-quality 3D concept render compatible with our game's visual language; avoid photorealistic grunge or excessive tiny greebles. Whole station and both flight corridors visible, no cropped modules, no diagram collage, no captions or watermark.
